Make It Yours!

custom products, made slowly

For years I’ve been doing this to my own photographs — tearing them apart, layering them, turning an ordinary afternoon into somewhere you’d actually want to live for a minute.

Make It Yours is that, for your photo. Send the one you can’t stop looking at — the dog, the kitchen table, your grandmother’s hands — and I’ll rebuild it in the same torn-paper world the cards live in. Illustrated. Animated. A little alive.

“Same hands, same scissors. Different afternoon.”

It isn’t open yet. I’m still working out how to do it properly — one at a time, slowly, without turning it into a factory.

You get your afternoon back, as a scrapbook world.

The Library

a shelf that grows

storybooks, not lectures

Children’s novels, grown from the mantras

Every mantra started as a lesson I had to learn the hard way. The Library is where those lessons become children’s novels — real stories, not sermons, each one built around a principle from the journey so far.

“Nothing to finish. Nothing due back.”

Simple enough for a kid to follow, honest enough for the adult reading it out loud. A shelf that grows one story at a time, in no particular order — I know what belongs on it now, I just haven’t built it yet.
