Print questions

what arrives, and how it is measured

What does the listed size describe?

The selected size describes the artwork print. The included 2-inch mat and 20mm Classic frame face make the finished piece larger; the 2D viewer lists print, glazed opening, and overall dimensions separately.

Will the finished size match the preview exactly?

Room and frame previews are visual guides. Prodigi may produce framed prints in inches or the nearest metric equivalent depending on production location, so finished dimensions can differ by a few millimeters. Use the dimensions confirmed with your order when planning wall space.

Are the pieces sold unframed?

No. Gallery pieces are offered framed and matted so the artwork arrives ready to hang in the presentation shown here.

Commission questions

how your photograph becomes a paper world

Is every commissioned piece original?

Yes. Nicolas rebuilds your photograph by hand as a one-of-one torn-paper artwork rather than applying a preset filter or reproducing an existing piece.

What happens after I submit the form?

Nicolas reviews the story, size, timing, and budget you shared, then replies personally. You’ll send the source photograph after that conversation. Nothing is charged or agreed until you both say yes.

How long does a commission take?

Timing depends on the image, scale, and current commission queue. Add your date to the inquiry—especially for a gift—and Nicolas will confirm what is realistically possible before work begins.

how the community corner grows

Can anyone buy a listing?

No. The Marketplace and business directory are selected by hand. Nobody pays for placement, and no person or business is listed without permission.

Who can submit a creation?

Independent creators and registered businesses may submit original work they made or hold the rights to sell. Clear product photos and proof of the work are required for review.

Does submitting guarantee acceptance?

No. Every submission is reviewed by a person, and Nicolas will email you either way. Pricing, availability, shipping responsibility, and any next steps are agreed only after a piece is accepted.
