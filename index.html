Nicolas took the picture, tore it up, layered it, glued it back into a little world of its own. The Gallery is where those worlds get to be big.

“Close enough to see the paper fibers, and the bit of glue that never dried flat.”

The pieces get hung with room to breathe. We’re still arguing about what goes on which wall.

Not a lecture. A conversation.

— the whole idea, honestly

To Bee Honest isn’t here to sell you a fix. It’s a quiet invitation: a one-on-one conversation with yourself, disguised as beautiful paper things.

Every image here started as a photograph Nicolas took — then tore, layered, and rebuilt by hand into scrapbook worlds. The tower of books? A real pile he photographed in Paris one December. Nothing stock. Nothing borrowed.

“We’re not selling products. We’re letting you know there’s a way to invest in your mental health — and feel better for a small fee.”
