# PRODUCT.md — To Bee Honest

## What this is
Brand site for **To Bee Honest, LLC** (Nicolas Bettinger, Panama City Beach FL). A mental-wellness universe built on self-connection: a book, a mantra card deck, a future podcast — later kombucha, protein balls, "a whole eclectic universe."

## Positioning (founder's words)
"We are not selling products — we're letting you know there is a way to invest in your mental health and feel better for a small fee." Coaching/mentorship hybrid in the Don Miguel Ruiz lane. Everything must feel **like a story or a conversation, never a lecture**.

## Products
- **The Sun Stone Theory** — book, self-help/affirmations. Sun-stone metaphor: inner perspectives help you "level up." Statements of identity, commitments, reframings, practical guidance ("Pause. Listen. Feel. Trust.")
- **Mantra card deck** — his own photos transformed into torn-paper scrapbook art + original mantras (e.g. leopard card: "Is people pleasing worth betraying myself?")
- **Podcast** — planned, on the mantra themes
- Future: kombucha, protein balls (mention lightly, don't build commerce)

## Audience
Curious people who feel energetically out of place; have potential they can't activate; feel victimized and want out; want peace. Skews women/non-binary, millennial + Gen X, drawn to short-form ritualized daily practice.

## Brand voice / themes
Playfulness, childlike excitement, passion, love, empowerment, community. Inclusive: "captain of the boat, but no one without the crew."

## Mode
Persuade (brand/landing). Success = visitor feels the world, wants the book/deck, joins the list.
